Tanipedia is an e-commerce platform designed to bridge the gap between local farmers and traders. Our mission is to provide an efficient and transparent marketplace where farmers can directly sell their agricultural products to traders and businesses, eliminating the need for middlemen.
This platform aims to help farmers gain greater control over their sales and pricing, while also providing traders with a reliable and direct source for fresh, high-quality produce.

Key Features:
Farmer Dashboard: Allows farmers to manage their product listings, track orders, and communicate directly with buyers.
Trader Marketplace: Provides a user-friendly interface for traders to browse and purchase a wide variety of agricultural products.
Transparent Pricing: Ensures fair and competitive prices for both farmers and traders.
